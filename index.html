NEED TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Still have questions? Speak directly with Bukola.

Chat on WhatsApp ↗
Will I keep driving my car during the loan?

No. The vehicle stays in E-XIN's custody throughout the loan period, so you will not be able to drive it during this time.

How much can I borrow?

The available amount depends on the vehicle assessment and your application. The team will confirm any offer after reviewing your details.

What documents will I need?

Be ready to provide identification and vehicle ownership documents. Your advisor will confirm the exact documents required for your vehicle.

What are the interest rate and repayment terms?

Interest, fees, loan duration and repayment schedule are confirmed after assessment and set out in the loan agreement. Review them carefully before accepting an offer.

Which locations are currently served?

Enquiries are currently accepted from Lagos, Ogun and Oyo. E-XIN will confirm availability for your exact area.

Does submitting the form mean my loan is approved?

No. The form is an enquiry only. Loan availability and approval are subject to assessment and confirmation by E-XIN Capital.
