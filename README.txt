E-XIN CAPITAL - NETLIFY WEBSITE

FILES
- index.html: page content and enquiry form
- style.css: responsive design and layout
- script.js: mobile navigation and WhatsApp enquiry form
- assets/: logo and hero image

DEPLOY TO NETLIFY
1. Keep the folder structure exactly as it is.
2. Sign in to Netlify and choose Add new site > Deploy manually (or drag-and-drop deploy).
3. Drag the entire exin-netlify-site folder, or upload the ZIP after extracting it locally if your Netlify screen requests a folder.
4. Netlify will publish the site and provide a URL.

WHATSAPP
The contact number is configured in index.html links and script.js as 2349022658730 (the international format for 09022658730).
To change it later, update all wa.me links in index.html and WHATSAPP_NUMBER in script.js.

FORM BEHAVIOUR
The enquiry form validates required fields and opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message to Bukola. The visitor must tap Send in WhatsApp. This static website does not store form submissions in a database and does not automatically submit or approve a loan.

IMPORTANT
Before publishing, verify the company's legal disclosures, service areas, eligibility criteria, custody wording, fees and lending terms. Replace any wording that does not match the company's approved policies. The page does not promise loan approval or a guaranteed amount.
